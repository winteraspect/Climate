# Academic References & Literature Bibliography

This repository builds upon established research at the intersection of **climate econometrics**, **meteorological anomalies (ENSO)**, and **commodity futures price dynamics**.

In compliance with academic publishing norms and copyright law, all works are cited formally with standard bibliographic metadata and official digital object identifiers (DOIs) rather than redistributing copyrighted full-text PDFs.
